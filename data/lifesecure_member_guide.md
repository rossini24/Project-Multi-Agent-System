# 1. About this guide

This guide describes the benefits, limits and procedures for members of LifeSecure Insurance holding a Base Plan or an Executive Plan. It is intended for members and for partner medical practices. In case of conflict, the policy contract prevails over this guide.

LifeSecure policy numbers use the prefix EX for Executive Plan members and BS for Base Plan members, followed by a four-digit number, for example EX-4471. Any request sent to LifeSecure must quote the policy number and the full name of the insured member.

# 2. Eligibility and enrollment

## 2.1 Who can be insured

Adults aged 18 and above can enroll individually. The Executive Plan also allows enrollment of a spouse and dependent children under a single policy number, listed as additional insured members on the same certificate.

## 2.2 Plan overview

| Benefit | Base Plan | Executive Plan |
|:-----------------|:----------------------|:----------------------|
| Annual full check-up | Reimbursement only, up to 50% | Direct billing, up to 500 euros |
| Individual specialist visits | Reimbursement, up to 40% | Reimbursement, up to 80% |
| Lipid profile (cholesterol test) | Included in the annual check-up only | Included in the annual check-up, plus 1 additional standalone test per year |
| Family members covered | Not applicable | Spouse and dependent children |
| Annual reimbursement ceiling | 300 euros | 2,000 euros |

# 3. The annual full check-up

## 3.1 Executive Plan

The annual full check-up is covered through direct billing with the practice, up to a cap of 500 euros. Within this cap, the member pays nothing at the time of the visit and no reimbursement paperwork is required. Amounts above the cap are invoiced to the member, who may then request reimbursement by submitting the original invoice within 60 days.

The annual full check-up under the Executive Plan includes a general examination, a complete blood count, a lipid profile, and a basic metabolic panel.

## 3.2 Base Plan

The annual full check-up is not covered through direct billing. The member pays the practice directly and can request reimbursement of up to 50% of the cost, capped at 150 euros per year. The reimbursement request must be submitted within 60 days of the visit, with the original invoice and the LifeSecure reimbursement form.

# 4. Standalone laboratory tests

## 4.1 Lipid profile requested outside the annual check-up

Under the Executive Plan, members are entitled to one additional standalone lipid profile per calendar year, beyond the one included in the annual check-up, reimbursed at 80% with no pre-authorization required. A prescription from any treating doctor is sufficient.

Under the Base Plan, a standalone lipid profile requested outside the annual check-up is not covered and is entirely at the member's expense.

## 4.2 Other standalone tests

Standalone blood tests other than the lipid profile (glucose, thyroid panel, and similar) are reimbursed at 60% under the Executive Plan, within the annual reimbursement ceiling, and are not covered under the Base Plan.

# 5. Specialist visits outside the check-up

## 5.1 Executive Plan

Individual specialist visits, including cardiology, endocrinology and dermatology, are reimbursed at 80% of the cost, within the annual reimbursement ceiling of 2,000 euros. No referral is required.

## 5.2 Base Plan

Individual specialist visits are reimbursed at 40% of the cost, within the annual reimbursement ceiling of 300 euros. A referral from a general practitioner is required for reimbursement to be approved.

# 6. Reimbursement procedure

## 6.1 Required documents

To request reimbursement, the member (or, on their behalf, the practice) submits: the original invoice, the LifeSecure reimbursement form, the policy number, and a brief description of the service received.

## 6.2 Timing

Reimbursement requests must be submitted within 60 days of the date of service. LifeSecure processes complete requests within 20 business days and transfers the reimbursed amount directly to the member's registered bank account.

## 6.3 Incomplete requests

A reimbursement request missing the original invoice or the policy number is returned to the sender without processing. LifeSecure does not accept photocopies of invoices as a substitute for the original.

# 7. Waiting periods and exclusions

## 7.1 Waiting periods

New members are subject to a 60-day waiting period from the activation date for all benefits described in this guide. The waiting period does not apply to members transferring directly from another LifeSecure policy without a coverage gap.

## 7.2 Exclusions

The following are excluded under both plans: cosmetic and elective procedures, pre-existing conditions not declared at enrollment, treatments received outside the country of issue except in documented emergencies, and any service for which the member cannot produce an original invoice.

# 8. Guidance for medical practices answering coverage enquiries

## 8.1 Information versus confirmation

This guide may be used to explain, in general terms, what a plan typically covers. A practice should not state that a specific visit or test will definitely be paid by LifeSecure for a specific member. Final confirmation of coverage is issued only by LifeSecure after verifying the member's policy status.

## 8.2 Verifying the insured member

Before discussing coverage details, the practice must confirm that the person named in the request is a registered patient and that the policy number matches internal records. Coverage information must not be shared with a sender whose identity or relationship to the policy cannot be confirmed.

## 8.3 When information is missing

If a plan, policy number, or specific service is not described in this guide, the practice should state that the information is not available internally and will need to be verified directly with LifeSecure, rather than guessing an answer.

# 9. Frequently asked questions

**Is the annual check-up covered under the Executive Plan?** Yes, through direct billing up to 500 euros; amounts above that are reimbursed after the fact with an invoice.

**Is a standalone cholesterol test covered under the Base Plan?** No, only the lipid profile included in the annual check-up is covered under the Base Plan.

**How long does reimbursement take?** 20 business days from a complete request.

**Can family members be added to the Base Plan?** No, only the Executive Plan allows additional insured family members.

**What happens if I submit a photocopy of the invoice?** The request is returned unprocessed; only the original invoice is accepted.
