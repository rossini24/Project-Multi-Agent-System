# 1. About this guide

This guide describes benefits, limits and procedures for members of WellCare Assurance holding an Individual Plan or a Family Plan. It is intended for members and for partner medical practices. Where this guide and the policy contract differ, the policy contract prevails.

WellCare Assurance policy numbers use the prefix WC followed by a five-digit number, for example WC-30442. Requests sent to WellCare must quote the policy number and the full name of the insured member.

# 2. Eligibility and enrollment

## 2.1 Who can be insured

The Individual Plan covers one adult. The Family Plan covers one or two adults and their dependent children under 18, all listed under the same policy number.

## 2.2 Plan overview

| Benefit | Individual Plan | Family Plan |
|:-----------------|:----------------------|:----------------------|
| General practitioner visits | 4 per year, 80% covered | 8 per year per family member, 80% covered |
| Cardiology check-up visits | 1 per year, reimbursement 70% | 1 per year per adult, reimbursement 70% |
| Lipid profile (cholesterol test) | 1 per year, 100% covered | 1 per year per insured member, 100% covered |
| Pediatric check-up visits | Not applicable | Included, 2 per year per child |
| Pre-authorization required | Only for advanced diagnostics | Only for advanced diagnostics |

# 3. Laboratory tests

## 3.1 Lipid profile (cholesterol test)

Both plans cover one lipid profile per year per insured member, at 100%, when prescribed by a doctor. No pre-authorization is required. A second lipid profile within the same year is covered at 50% only if a cardiovascular risk condition is documented by the prescribing doctor.

## 3.2 Other routine blood tests

Complete blood count and glucose tests are covered at 90% under both plans, up to six tests per year per insured member.

## 3.3 Advanced diagnostics

Tumor markers, hormonal panels and specialized imaging require written pre-authorization under both plans, processed within five business days.

# 4. Specialist visits

## 4.1 Cardiology check-up visits

Cardiology check-up visits are reimbursed at 70% under both plans, one visit per year per insured adult, with no pre-authorization required. The member pays the practice directly and requests reimbursement afterward.

## 4.2 Pediatric check-up visits

Pediatric check-up visits are available only under the Family Plan, covered at 100%, up to two visits per year for each insured child. They are not available under the Individual Plan, which does not cover dependants.

## 4.3 Other specialist visits

Dermatology and endocrinology visits are reimbursed at 60% under both plans, within an annual ceiling of 350 euros per insured member.

# 5. Pre-authorization

## 5.1 When it is required

Pre-authorization is required only for advanced diagnostics listed in section 3.3. It is not required for cardiology check-ups, lipid profiles, or general practitioner visits under either plan.

## 5.2 How to request it

The request is sent to the WellCare claims desk with the policy number, the member's name, the type of service and the prescribing doctor. WellCare responds within five business days.

# 6. Waiting periods and exclusions

## 6.1 Waiting periods

A waiting period of 45 days applies to all benefits from the activation date, with no exceptions for check-up visits or laboratory tests.

## 6.2 Exclusions

The following are excluded under both plans: cosmetic treatments, elective surgery, conditions known and undeclared at enrollment, and treatments received before the activation date or during the waiting period.

# 7. Billing and reimbursement

## 7.1 Reimbursement procedure

WellCare operates primarily on a reimbursement basis: the member pays the practice and submits the original invoice together with the WellCare reimbursement form within 45 days of the visit. WellCare transfers the reimbursed amount within 15 business days of receiving a complete request.

## 7.2 Direct billing

Direct billing is not available under either WellCare plan at this time. All services are paid by the member first and reimbursed afterward according to the percentages in this guide.

# 8. Guidance for medical practices answering coverage enquiries

## 8.1 Information versus confirmation

This guide can be used to explain what a WellCare plan generally covers. A practice must not confirm that a specific service will be reimbursed for a specific member; only WellCare can confirm this, after verifying the policy.

## 8.2 Verifying the insured member

Before discussing any member's coverage, the practice must verify that the person is a registered patient and that the policy number matches internal records. Coverage details must not be disclosed to a sender whose identity cannot be confirmed.

## 8.3 When information is missing

If the plan, policy number, or requested service is not described in this guide, the practice should state that the information is not available internally and will be verified directly with WellCare, rather than guessing.

# 9. Frequently asked questions

**Is a cholesterol test covered under the Individual Plan?** Yes, one lipid profile per year, at 100%, with no pre-authorization.

**Are pediatric check-ups covered under the Individual Plan?** No, only the Family Plan covers dependent children.

**Does WellCare offer direct billing?** No, all plans work on a reimbursement basis.

**How long does reimbursement take?** 15 business days from a complete request.

**Is pre-authorization needed for a cardiology check-up?** No, only for advanced diagnostics.
