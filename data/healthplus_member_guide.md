# 1. About this guide

This guide describes the benefits, limits and procedures that apply to members of HealthPlus Insurance who hold a Base Plan or a Premium Plan. It is written for members and for the medical practices that treat them. Where this guide and the policy contract differ, the policy contract prevails.

HealthPlus Insurance policy numbers begin with the prefix HP followed by a number, for example HP-2291. Every request sent to HealthPlus by a medical practice must quote the policy number and the full name of the insured member.

# 2. Eligibility and enrollment

## 2.1 Who can be insured

Adults aged 18 to 75 residing in the country of issue can enroll. Children can be added to a Premium Plan only, as dependants of an insured adult. A member is considered enrolled from the activation date printed on the policy certificate, and benefits are available only after the waiting period in section 6 has elapsed.

## 2.2 Plan overview

| Benefit | Base Plan | Premium Plan |
|:-----------------|:----------------------|:----------------------|
| General practitioner visits | 6 per year, 70% covered | Unlimited, 100% covered |
| Cardiology check-up visits | 1 per six-month period | Up to 2 per six-month period |
| Pre-authorization for cardiology check-ups | Required, in writing | Not required |
| Lipid profile (cholesterol blood test) | 1 per calendar year | 2 per calendar year |
| Pediatric check-up visits | Not included | Included, maximum 4 per year |
| Annual premium tier | Standard | Enhanced |

# 3. Specialist visits

## 3.1 Cardiology check-up visits under the Base Plan

The Base Plan includes one cardiology check-up visit per six-month period. The visit is covered only if HealthPlus has sent a written pre-authorization to the member before the visit takes place. A visit performed without a written pre-authorization is not covered, and the practice bills the cost directly to the patient.

## 3.2 Cardiology check-up visits under the Premium Plan

The Premium Plan includes up to two cardiology check-up visits per six-month period. No pre-authorization is required for these visits. The practice can proceed directly with the appointment once the member's policy number has been verified.

## 3.3 Other specialist visits

Endocrinology, dermatology and other specialist visits are covered at 60% under the Base Plan and 90% under the Premium Plan, up to an annual ceiling of 400 euros for the Base Plan and 1,200 euros for the Premium Plan. A referral from the member's general practitioner is required for the Base Plan.

## 3.4 Pediatric check-up visits

Pediatric check-up visits are not included in the Base Plan. Under the Premium Plan, routine pediatric check-up visits are included, up to a maximum of four visits per year for each insured child.

# 4. Laboratory tests

## 4.1 Lipid profile (cholesterol test)

A lipid profile measures total cholesterol, HDL and LDL cholesterol, and triglycerides. It requires fasting of at least eight hours.

Under the Base Plan, one lipid profile per calendar year is covered at 100% when it is prescribed by the member's general practitioner. No pre-authorization is needed for this first annual test. Additional lipid profiles within the same year are covered only if the prescribing doctor documents a cardiovascular risk diagnosis, and in that case a written pre-authorization is required.

Under the Premium Plan, two lipid profiles per calendar year are covered at 100%, without pre-authorization and without a documented risk diagnosis.

## 4.2 Other routine blood tests

Complete blood count, glucose and other routine blood tests are covered at 100% under the Premium Plan when prescribed. Under the Base Plan they are covered at 70%, with a maximum of eight routine tests per year.

## 4.3 Advanced diagnostics

Tumor markers, hormonal panels and microbiology cultures require written pre-authorization under both plans. Requests are processed within three business days of receipt of a complete request.

# 5. Pre-authorization

## 5.1 How to request it

A pre-authorization request is sent by the member or by the practice to the HealthPlus claims desk. It must contain the policy number, the member's full name, the type of service, the name of the prescribing doctor and the planned date. HealthPlus replies in writing within three business days.

## 5.2 Validity

A pre-authorization is valid for 60 days from the date of issue and applies to one specific service. If the service is not performed within 60 days, a new request is needed.

## 5.3 What pre-authorization does not guarantee

A pre-authorization confirms that the service is eligible under the plan. It does not guarantee payment if the member's policy was suspended, if the waiting period had not elapsed, or if information supplied in the request turns out to be inaccurate.

# 6. Waiting periods and exclusions

## 6.1 Waiting periods

A waiting period of 30 days applies to all benefits from the activation date. Cardiology visits and advanced diagnostics have an additional waiting period of 90 days under the Base Plan and 30 days under the Premium Plan.

## 6.2 Exclusions

The following are not covered under any plan: cosmetic treatments, dental care, treatments considered experimental, conditions that were known and not declared at enrollment, and services received before the activation date.

# 7. Billing and reimbursement

## 7.1 Direct billing

Where the practice has a direct billing agreement, covered amounts are invoiced to HealthPlus and the member pays only the portion not covered. The practice must first verify the policy number and the plan. Direct billing is available for the services listed in the plan overview when all conditions in this guide are met.

## 7.2 Reimbursement after the fact

When direct billing does not apply, the member pays the practice and requests reimbursement from HealthPlus within 90 days of the visit, attaching the original invoice and the reimbursement form. Reimbursement is paid within 30 days of receipt of a complete file.

# 8. Guidance for medical practices answering coverage enquiries

## 8.1 Information versus confirmation

A practice may explain what a plan generally includes, using this guide. A practice must not confirm that a specific visit or test will be paid by HealthPlus. Confirmation of coverage for a specific member is issued only by HealthPlus, in writing, after verification of the policy.

## 8.2 Verifying the insured member

Before discussing any member's coverage, the practice must check that the person is registered as a patient and that the policy number matches the records held by the practice. Coverage details of a member must never be disclosed to a third party whose identity cannot be verified.

## 8.3 When information is missing

If the plan, the policy number or the requested service is not covered by this guide, the practice must state that the information is not available internally and that it will be verified directly with HealthPlus. The practice must not guess or invent conditions.

# 9. Frequently asked questions

**Is a cholesterol test covered under the Base Plan?** Yes, one lipid profile per calendar year, at 100%, when prescribed by the general practitioner, with no pre-authorization.

**Do I need pre-authorization for a cholesterol test under the Premium Plan?** No.

**Is my cardiology check-up covered if I did not obtain a pre-authorization?** Under the Base Plan it is not covered. Under the Premium Plan no pre-authorization is needed.

**How long does a pre-authorization take?** Three business days from a complete request.

**How long can I wait before claiming a reimbursement?** 90 days from the date of the visit.
