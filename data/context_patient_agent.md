# FAQ and Policies — Medical Practice (context for PatientAgent)

## Opening hours

The practice is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM-7:00 PM. Saturdays by appointment only for specialist visits, 9:00 AM-1:00 PM. Closed Sundays and public holidays.

## Booking appointments

Appointments can be booked by phone or by replying to this email, with at least 48 hours notice to guarantee availability. For urgent requests (not medical emergencies, but a need to be seen within 24-48 hours), please state this clearly and the front desk will prioritize the reply.

## Cancelling and rescheduling appointments

Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 24 hours before the scheduled time. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice, or no-shows without notice, incur a 20 euro fee, except in cases of documented personal emergency.

To reschedule, please indicate the original appointment (date, time, doctor) and at least two alternative preferred time slots within the same week or the following one.

## Documents required for a first visit

For a first visit with any specialist at the practice, patients must bring:
- valid ID
- health insurance card
- any prior clinical documentation relevant to the reason for the visit (reports, previous tests, letters from other doctors)
- a list of currently taken medications, if any

For follow-up visits after the first one, the health insurance card is sufficient, unless otherwise indicated by the doctor.

## Payment methods

The practice accepts cash, card payments (POS terminal available at the front desk), and bank transfer for pre-agreed visit packages arranged with the doctor. An invoice or receipt is always issued, and can be provided electronically via email upon request.

## Clinical requests via email

Front desk staff and automated assistants at the practice are not authorized to provide diagnoses, symptom interpretations, or treatment advice via email, regardless of who is asking. Any question of a clinical nature must be redirected to a consultation with the doctor, by phone or in person depending on perceived urgency.

## Situations requiring immediate attention

Symptoms such as intense chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, sudden swelling of the face/throat, loss of consciousness, or uncontrollable bleeding must be treated as potential emergencies: the patient should be directed immediately to the emergency number or the nearest emergency room, not to a regular booking.
