# Insurance policies (context for InsuranceAgent)

## Company: HealthPlus Insurance

### Base Plan
- Specialist check-up visits: 1 semi-annual cardiology check-up included, requires **written pre-authorization** sent by the insurance company before the visit.
- Without pre-authorization, the visit is not covered and is billed to the patient.
- Pediatric visits: not included.

### Premium Plan
- Specialist check-up visits: up to 2 semi-annual cardiology check-ups included, **no pre-authorization required**.
- Pediatric check-up visits: included, maximum 4 per year.

## Company: LifeSecure Insurance

### Executive Plan
- Annual full check-up: covered through **direct billing** with the practice, up to a cap of 500 euros. No reimbursement documentation required for amounts within the cap.
- For amounts exceeding the cap, the patient receives an invoice for the difference and can request reimbursement from the insurer by submitting the original invoice.
- Individual specialist visits (not check-ups): reimbursed after the fact, requires invoice and LifeSecure reimbursement request form within 60 days of the visit.

### Base Plan (LifeSecure)
- Annual full check-up: not included in direct billing, reimbursement only after the fact up to 50% of the cost, maximum 150 euros/year.

## Note for the agent

If a request concerns a company or plan not covered in this document, **do not invent a clause**: reply that the specific information is not available internally and will be verified directly with the insurance company, optionally asking for the policy number so the front desk can check manually.
